3. CONNECTIONS BETWEEN GESTURAL ACTIONS AND SOUNDS

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAPPING 1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ACTION:
Hold the phone upright and make a clear upward thrust.

SOUND:
A single sci-fi torpedo launch is triggered.

ADDITIONAL DETAILS:
The phone may deviate by about 25 degrees from vertical. The upward
acceleration threshold is 3, the phone must first remain steady for 300 ms,
and an 800 ms cooldown prevents repeated launches. /torpedo/trigger stays
at 1 for 100 ms before returning to 0.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAPPING 2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ACTION:
Hold the phone flat and rotate it horizontally, with the screen facing
either up or down.

SOUND:
A continuous engine sound becomes busier as the phone rotates faster.

ADDITIONAL DETAILS:
The phone may deviate by about 20 degrees from flat. It must remain nearly
still for 300 ms before the control is armed. The engine starts at 40
degrees/second, stops at 30 degrees/second, and reaches full activity at
240 degrees/second. Rotation activity from 0 to 1 controls
/engine/maxSpeed; /engine/volume remains fixed at 1.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------
MAPPING 3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------

ACTION:
Tilt the phone left or right.

SOUND:
A brass sound becomes stronger as the tilt angle increases.

ADDITIONAL DETAILS:
Tilts from 0 to 5 degrees form a silent dead zone. From 5 to 45 degrees,
the tilt is mapped linearly to /brass/blower/pressure from 0 to 1. At 45
degrees or more, the pressure remains at its maximum value of 1.
